Offline Data Sources & As-Of Reference Dates

ZERO RUNTIME NETWORK CALLS

In strict adherence to ASTRAQUANT X 2026 rules, the running AlphaWatch AI website operates in an air-gapped offline state. It never calls the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) or Screener.in during web runtime. All market intelligence is deterministically computed from saved local files:

01

Sanitized Ingestion Layer

Parses NSE CM-UDiFF and legacy Bhavcopy files, isolates Series EQ, checks chronological order, removes duplicates, and flags price jumps >20% for unadjusted corporate actions. Excel files are opened with data_only=True, stripping formula prefixes (=, +, -, @) to eliminate CSV/formula injection threats.

02

Leak-Free Feature Engineering

Calculates 20 quantitative features strictly from past data (1/5/20d returns, SMA/EMA distances for 5/20/50, RSI 14, 20d volatility, volume ratios, delivery trends, NIFTY return context, VIX level, and 52-week high distance). Zero lookahead bias mathematically proven via unit testing.

03

Walk-Forward Model Training

5-fold expanding walk-forward cross validation. Scalers are fitted strictly on training data. Calibrated Random Forest and Logistic Regression classifiers estimate upward directional probabilities, serialized into models/saved/ alongside audit metrics JSON files.

04

6-Pillar Diagnostic & 2x2 Matrix

Rule-based fundamental evaluation (Strong, Mixed, Weak) across Valuation, Profitability, Efficiency, Solvency, Growth, and Ownership. Combines with ML predictions into transparent educational research summaries (e.g., "Model says BUY, fundamentals Weak: review before adding").

Backtesting & Friction Modeling

To avoid the rampant trap of backtest overfitting, AlphaWatch AI enforces:

  • Next-Day Open Execution: Signals evaluated on Day t Close are executed strictly at Day t+1 Open.
  • Realistic Friction: 0.10% Securities Transaction Tax (STT), 0.03% Brokerage, SEBI turnover fees, Stamp Duty, GST, and 0.05% slippage.
  • Long-Only Constraints: Cash equity spot modeling without unhedged naked shorting.
  • Audit Accountability: The Claim Auditor verifies every prediction against actual subsequent 5-day and 20-day returns.
