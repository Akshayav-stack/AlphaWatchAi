LEGAL & PRIVACY FRAMEWORK

Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Complete clarity on local data privacy, offline architecture, and instant Right to Erasure.

1. Privacy Policy & Local Data Sovereignty

ZERO EXTERNAL TELEMETRY

AlphaWatch AI is engineered with a strict Privacy-by-Design architecture:

  • Local Storage Only: All user credentials, portfolio holdings, risk profiles, and paper trading ledgers are stored solely within the local SQLite database on your host machine.
  • No External CDN or Telemetry: No external analytics (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.) or external CDN scripts are included. Even visualization libraries (Plotly.js) are hosted locally.
  • Secure Password Hashing: User passwords are hashed using state-of-the-art Argon2id with high memory and iteration parameters. Raw passwords are never persisted.
  • Encrypted Session Cookies: Authentication state is held in signed, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax cookies with automated cryptographic tampering defense.

2. Right to Erasure (Complete Account Deletion)

CASCADING PERMANENT DELETE

In compliance with strict data protection principles (GDPR Article 17 and Digital Personal Data Protection Act):

  • Every user has unconditional access to the "Delete My Account" action on the Settings page.
  • Triggering account deletion initiates an immediate SQL cascading delete: your user record, holdings, investor profile, and paper trading history are purged permanently and irrecoverably from the database.
  • No ghost records, cached profile weights, or soft-deleted identifiers are retained.
