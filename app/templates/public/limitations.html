MANDATORY RESEARCH DISCLOSURES

Limitations & Academic Disclaimer

Full architectural transparency regarding system scope, security boundaries, and operational boundaries.

Educational Research Signal Notice

AlphaWatch AI is strictly an academic research demonstration for ASTRAQUANT X 2026. It does NOT constitute investment advice, financial planning, portfolio management services, or SEBI-registered research recommendations. All outputs are educational quantitative experiments.

1. No Broker Integration / No Real Orders

AIR-GAPPED

AlphaWatch AI contains zero broker API credentials, zero trading gateways, and zero real order routing. No trade placed on this platform reaches Zerodha, Groww, Upstox, Angel One, or any exchange member. All executions on the Paper Trading page are mathematical simulations recorded solely within your local SQLite session.

2. No Email Verification & No Password Reset

OFFLINE ARCHITECTURE

Because AlphaWatch AI is designed to run 100% offline without external network dependencies, there is no SMTP email dispatch server configured. Consequently:

  • There is no email verification during registration.
  • There is no automated "Forgot Password" or password reset mechanism.
  • Users are responsible for recording their credentials. If forgotten, accounts can be safely re-registered.

3. No Admin Area / No Backdoors

ZERO PRIVILEGED ROLES

There is no administrative backend, superuser dashboard, or master credential. Every database query enforces strict user-level parameterization (WHERE user_id = :id). No user or system operator can view or modify another user's holdings, risk scores, or trading logs.

4. Unadjusted Historical Corporate Actions

DATA CAVEAT

Raw NSE Bhavcopy archives reflect official end-of-day traded prices and are not retroactively adjusted for stock splits, rights issues, or bonus shares. To protect model integrity:

  • AlphaWatch AI automatically flags single-day price jumps >20% in data_loader.py.
  • Users are advised that historical returns around split dates reflect raw nominal prices.

5. Screener.in As-Of Staleness Threshold

120-DAY WINDOW

Fundamental ratios (P/E, ROE, ROCE, Debt to Equity, Sales & Profit Growth) depend on published quarterly and annual filings. AlphaWatch AI displays the exact export date from Screener.in and surfaces an explicit "Stale Data Warning" whenever the export date is older than 120 days.

6. Probabilistic Signals & Regime Shifts

PROBABILISTIC

Machine learning predictions represent the estimated empirical probability that Close(t+1) > Close(t). Financial markets are non-stationary with fat-tailed risk distributions. Past algorithmic precision does not guarantee future outcomes during unexpected geopolitical or macroeconomic regime shifts.
